Cosmic Structures

Nebulae & Star Nurseries

Vast clouds of dust and gas where gravity triggers the birth of new stellar systems across light-years.

Exoplanetary Systems

Distant worlds orbiting foreign suns, ranging from scorching hot Jupiters to ocean-covered super-Earths.

Supernovae & Relics

The explosive deaths of giant stars that synthesize heavy elements and leave behind neutron stars or black holes.

Mapping the Observable Universe

Astronomy bridges physics, chemistry, and observational science to reveal how energy and matter evolve over billions of years. Modern orbital telescopes continue to expand our visible horizon, capturing light that traveled across space before Earth was formed.

Quick Facts

  • Stellar Scale Estimated ~200 billion trillion stars in the observable universe.
  • Light Speed Travels at roughly 299,792 kilometers per second.
  • Cosmic Background CMB radiation dates back ~380,000 years after the Big Bang.
